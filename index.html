Inter Concept Space

A framework for exploring how ideas connect, combine, evolve and shape the worlds created by humans and intelligent systems.

Definition

Inter Concept Space is the proposed landscape formed by relationships among concepts. Rather than viewing ideas as isolated entities, it treats them as nodes within a connected structure that gives rise to meaning, knowledge, culture and intelligence.

The Core Question

Why do similar patterns appear in mythology, science, mathematics, law and artificial intelligence?
Can concepts be mapped like locations in a landscape?
How do new ideas emerge from existing ideas?
Can intelligent systems navigate conceptual space the way humans navigate physical space?

A World of Relationships

Justice gains meaning through its relationship to law. Law gains meaning through society. Society gains meaning through identity. Identity gains meaning through memory. Memory gains meaning through language. Language gains meaning through knowledge.


Concepts rarely exist alone. Meaning emerges from connections.

Why Now?

Modern AI systems learn internal representations that organize concepts into high-dimensional spaces. Knowledge graphs connect entities through relationships. Human cognition appears to organize information through networks of associations. Inter Concept Space explores whether these observations point toward a deeper structure underlying thought, culture and intelligence.
