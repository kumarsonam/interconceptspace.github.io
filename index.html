Inter Concept Space

Exploring how ideas connect, evolve and generate meaning.

What is Inter Concept Space?

Inter Concept Space is a proposed framework for understanding how concepts connect, evolve and generate meaning. Rather than viewing ideas as isolated entities, it treats them as parts of a connected landscape where knowledge, culture, institutions and intelligent systems emerge from relationships between concepts.

Why Now?

Large language models, knowledge graphs and modern cognitive science all suggest that intelligence may arise from networks of relationships rather than isolated facts. Inter Concept Space explores whether concepts themselves form a deeper structure that can be studied, mapped and navigated.
