Inter Concept Space

Mapping the Landscape of Ideas

What is Inter Concept Space?

Inter Concept Space is a proposed framework for understanding how concepts connect, evolve and generate meaning.

Rather than viewing ideas as isolated entities, it treats them as parts of a connected landscape. Knowledge, culture, science, institutions and intelligent systems emerge from relationships between concepts.

Core Questions

How do ideas relate to one another?
How do new concepts emerge?
Can concepts be mapped like locations?
Can AI navigate conceptual space?

Why It Matters

Modern AI systems learn internal representations that organize concepts into structured spaces. Knowledge graphs connect information through relationships. Human cognition appears to operate through networks of associations.

Inter Concept Space explores whether these observations point toward a deeper structure underlying thought, meaning and intelligence.
