Inter Concept Space

A proposal that ideas are not isolated objects, but parts of a connected landscape through which minds, cultures, institutions and intelligent systems navigate.

The Core Question

Why do similar patterns appear in science, mathematics, mythology, law, language, technology and human behaviour?

Inter Concept Space explores the possibility that concepts do not exist independently. Instead, every concept derives meaning from its relationships with other concepts.

A World of Relationships

Justice connects to law. Law connects to society. Society connects to identity. Identity connects to memory. Memory connects to language. Language connects to knowledge.

Viewed together, concepts form a network rather than a collection of isolated definitions.

Vision

To develop a framework for understanding how ideas connect, evolve, combine and influence the worlds created by humans and intelligent systems.
